# Research and technical references

## Three supplied research papers

[1] Bei Xu and Yifan Xu. 2024. **Personalized Review Recommendation based on Implicit Dimension Mining.** In *Proceedings of the 2024 Conference of the North American Chapter of the Association for Computational Linguistics: Human Language Technologies (Volume 2: Short Papers)*, pages 86-91. Association for Computational Linguistics. The paper proposes PRR-LI, mines implicit dimensions from text and requirements, and combines text with dimensions for representation. https://aclanthology.org/2024.naacl-short.8/

[2] Lei Li, Yongfeng Zhang, and Li Chen. 2021. **Personalized Transformer for Explainable Recommendation.** Published as a conference paper at ACL-IJCNLP 2021. arXiv:2105.11601. The paper proposes PETER, using user/item identifiers and a context-prediction task for personalized explanation generation, while also predicting recommendations. https://aclanthology.org/2021.acl-long.383/ (preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.11601)

[3] Yixin Zhang, Sichen Lin, Zhili Zhao, Xuran Zhu, and Chenbo He. 2025. **Personalized recommendation by integrating a neural topic model and Bayesian personalized ranking.** *Knowledge-Based Systems*, 311, 113110. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.knosys.2025.113110
